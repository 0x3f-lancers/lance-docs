## What the bot keeps

To run your account, Lance keeps:

- your Telegram user ID and username, and which community's bot you use;
- your connected wallet address;
- your encrypted trading key, until it's replaced or you remove access;
- your orders and fills placed through the bot;
- your settings, such as order size, leverage and time zone;
- for each Lance Trade browser: the device, a city and country, and a partly hidden IP address.

## What your community owner can see

Owners see their community's totals, and their top traders by volume with each one's Telegram username, wallet address and a link to their public record on Hyperliquid. Their export lists every fill through their bot, with the Telegram user and wallet. See [Stats and exports](/owners/stats).

Trades on Hyperliquid are public: anyone can look up a wallet's trades on Hyperliquid's explorer.
