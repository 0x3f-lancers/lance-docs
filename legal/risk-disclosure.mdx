**Trading perpetual futures with leverage is high risk.** Prices can move fast and you can lose all the money you put in.

**Lance is software.** It does not give investment advice, manage money or trade on anyone's behalf. You make every trade yourself.

**Trades are executed on Hyperliquid**, a third-party exchange that is not affiliated with Lance.

**Lance is not available** to persons in the United States, the United Kingdom and sanctioned countries. See [Restricted regions](/legal/restricted-regions).

**Nothing on this site is an offer or solicitation** where it would be unlawful.

## Risks to understand

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Leverage and liquidation">
    Leverage makes gains and losses larger. With leverage, a small price move against you can wipe out your margin, and Hyperliquid can liquidate your position.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fast markets">
    Prices can gap. Market orders, take profits and stop losses can fill worse than expected, or not fill at all.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Software and connections">
    Lance, Telegram, your browser, your wallet or Hyperliquid can be slow or unavailable. Orders may fail, and alerts may arrive late or not at all.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Third-party exchange">
    Your funds and positions are on Hyperliquid. Lance doesn't control Hyperliquid, its markets, its prices or its availability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Testnet">
    Lance runs on Hyperliquid testnet for now. Testnet funds have no value, and testnet can behave differently from mainnet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
