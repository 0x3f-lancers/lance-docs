## 6. Your wallet and your Lance trading key

To trade, you connect your wallet on connect.lancetrade.xyz. Connecting works like this:

1. Lance creates a new trading key for you. Hyperliquid calls this an "API wallet".
2. Your wallet signs a message that proves the wallet is yours. This signature cannot move funds.
3. Your wallet approves the trading key on Hyperliquid, and approves the Lance fee at the rate shown on the page.

On Hyperliquid, the trading key can place and cancel orders, change leverage, set take-profit and stop-loss orders, and move funds between your own Hyperliquid balances, sub-accounts and vaults. Hyperliquid does not let it withdraw your funds or send them to another wallet. It stops working about 180 days after you approve it. The bot tries to remind you before then, and you can renew it with one signature. We store the key encrypted, and our software uses it only for orders you ask for or set up.

## 7. Placing orders

- Before you open or close a position, Lance shows the market, direction, size, leverage and fee, and waits for you to confirm. - Market orders fill at the best available price within a slippage limit. If the price moves past that limit, the order may fill in part or not at all. Limit orders wait on Hyperliquid's order book until they fill or are cancelled.
