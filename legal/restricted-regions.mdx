Lance isn't available to persons in:

- the **United States**;
- the **United Kingdom**;
- **sanctioned countries**.

If you're in one of these places, don't use Lance, and don't use a VPN or any other way to get around this.

This list can change. Check this page and the [Terms of Service](https://lancers.technology/legal?tab=tos) for the current list.
