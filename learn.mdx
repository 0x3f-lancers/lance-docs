**Lance Learn** is a free course for people new to perps. It needs no sign-in and no wallet.

- **In Lance Trade:** open [Learn](https://app.lancetrade.xyz/learn) from the menu.
- **In your community's bot:** send `/learn`, or tap **📚 New to perps? Learn first** on the welcome screen.

## What's in it

32 short lessons in six parts:

| Part | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Start here** | What Lance is, how connecting works, and what trading costs |
| **Perps basics** | Long and short, leverage, liquidation and funding, and why perps are risky |
| **Placing trades** | Market and limit orders, the order book, sizing, take profit and stop loss |
| **Reading charts** | Candles, levels and every indicator on Lance Trade's chart |
| **Risk and discipline** | How much to risk, the limits you can set, and trading psychology |
| **Community tools** | Following and copying, price alerts, your stats and the leaderboard |

Each lesson ends with its key points, common mistakes, and a short check to test yourself. There's also a glossary of the terms you'll meet.

Your progress is saved in your browser only.

<Note>
  Lessons explain how things work. They aren't advice on what to trade.
</Note>
