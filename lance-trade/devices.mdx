Each browser you sign in to Lance Trade gets its own session, which lasts 30 days.

## Every new sign-in is announced

When a browser signs in, your bot sends a message naming the device and roughly where it signed in from, with a **Sign out this browser** button. If you don't recognise it, tap that button straight away.

## See your devices

In your bot, open **🔐 Wallet and access** and tap **🖥 Devices**. For each browser you see:

- when and where it signed in;
- when and where it was last used;
- when its session ends;
- a button to sign it out.

**Sign out everywhere** ends every session at once. Devices and signing out work even while your community's bot is paused.

## When sessions end

A session ends when:

- 30 days have passed;
- you sign out in Lance Trade, or sign that browser out from **🖥 Devices**;
- you tap **Sign out everywhere**;
- you remove access or connect a different wallet in the bot.

## About locations

Places are a city and country, worked out from the browser's IP address with an offline database ([DB-IP](https://db-ip.com), CC BY 4.0). Addresses are never sent anywhere to be looked up. Only a partly hidden form of the address is kept, such as `49.36.x.x`.
