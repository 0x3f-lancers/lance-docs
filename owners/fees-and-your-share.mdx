## The bot fee

Members pay Lance's bot fee of **0.05%** of the position size on each trade (illustrative; final fees are confirmed before launch). It comes on top of Hyperliquid's own trading fees, and members see it before every trade.

## Your share

You get **50% of the qualifying Lance bot fees** actually collected from trades placed through your bot. That includes take profits and stop losses the bot placed for your members.

Your share doesn't apply to Hyperliquid's own trading fees, funding or deposits. Final terms are agreed with you before launch.

## An example

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Active traders per month | 100 |
| Average volume per trader per month | $25,000 |
| Monthly trading volume | $2,500,000 |
| Bot fees at 0.05% | $1,250 |
| **Your 50% share** | **$625** |

<Note>
  Illustration only. What you earn depends on how much your members trade.
</Note>

## Payouts

Your share is worked out from the fees collected on your community's trades. We pay it on the schedule agreed with you before launch.

## Check it yourself

Every fill and fee settles on Hyperliquid, so you can check your share against public data:

- `/stats` in your bot shows your volume, bot fees and share. See [Stats and exports](/owners/stats).
- **📥 Export CSV** gives you every fill with its Hyperliquid fill and order IDs, to check one by one.
