As an owner, send `/stats` to your own bot, or tap **🤝 Partner dashboard** on Home. Only owners see it.

## In your bot

| Figure | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **📊 Volume** | Trading volume through your bot, and over the last 24 hours |
| **💰 Bot fees** | Lance bot fees from those trades |
| **👤 Your share** | Your part of those bot fees |
| **👥 Members** | People who have started your bot, and how many connected a wallet |
| **📈 Traders** | Members who have traded, and how many this week |
| **🧾 Fills** | Fills recorded through your bot |

Below that are your top traders by volume, each with their wallet address, Telegram username and a link to their public record on Hyperliquid, and the builder address to check the fees on Hyperliquid yourself (**📋 Builder address** copies it).

From the dashboard you also reach [🎛 Community settings](/owners/bot-settings), **👤 Members** and **💬 Comments** ([Members and moderation](/owners/moderation)).

## Export every fill

Tap **📥 Export CSV** for a spreadsheet of every fill recorded through your bot, oldest first, with times in UTC. Each row has the Telegram user, the wallet, the fees, your share, and the Hyperliquid fill and order IDs, so you can check each one against Hyperliquid's public data.

## Community dashboard in Lance Trade

Tap **🌐 Open on the web** for a one-time sign-in link straight to **Community** in [Lance Trade](https://app.lancetrade.xyz). Like any Lance Trade sign-in, it needs your wallet connected in the bot. Community shows the same records on a bigger screen, refreshed every minute:

- **Needs attention**: a pause, members whose trading keys have expired or run out within 14 days, members who started the bot but never connected, members outside your linked group, each with a way to act.
- **Figures** for 7, 30 or 90 days: your share of the bot fees against the period before, volume, traders over 30 days, and members.
- **Day by day**: volume, your share, bot fees, traders or new members per UTC day, as a chart or a table, and **From start to trade**: how many members started the bot, connected, traded, and traded in the last 30 days.
- **Members**, 25 a page: Telegram username, wallet, where they stand, when they joined and last traded, volume, fills and your share. Search by username or wallet, filter (traded, connected, not connected, key running out, banned) and sort.
- **Markets** your members traded, the **latest trades**, **Check every fee** (the builder address, linked to Hyperliquid), and your **community's setup**, with where to change it in the bot.
- **Export CSV**: the same file as in the bot.

Days are UTC days, as on Hyperliquid.

<Warning>
  Stats and exports contain your members' Telegram usernames, wallet addresses and trading activity. That's personal data: keep it private, and don't post it or share it outside your team.
</Warning>
