<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your identity">
    Send us your community's name, your logo and a short welcome message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We configure your bot">
    We create your bot in Telegram and set it up under your name, with the markets and community settings you choose. See [Your bot's settings](/owners/bot-settings) for what you can set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You become an owner">
    We send you a one-time owner invite link. Open it in your bot and you become an owner, with your stats in the bot. The link works once, within 7 days. Your community can have more than one owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Members connect and review permissions">
    Share your bot's link with your community. Each member connects their wallet once and reviews the trading and fee permissions. See [Connect your wallet](/telegram/connect-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the trading fees">
    You receive 50% of Lance's bot fee on your community's trades. See [Fees and your share](/owners/fees-and-your-share).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How long it takes

Timing depends on your branding and setup. [Book a demo](https://telegram.me/bhargav2495) and we'll work through the steps and timeline with you.

## Before you share the bot

- **Post the bot's exact link** in your channel, and pin it. Impersonator bots copy names and logos, so members should only use the link you post.
- **Tell members it's on testnet.** While Lance runs on Hyperliquid testnet, trades use test funds.
- **Point them to these docs.** The [Quickstart](/quickstart) covers everything a member needs.
- **Don't promise results.** Trading perps is high risk. Don't tell members they'll make money, and share the [risk disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure).
