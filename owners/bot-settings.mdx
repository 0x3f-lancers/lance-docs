The Lance team sets these for you. Message us with what you want changed, and it applies to your members' next message.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Your bot's name in Telegram. Changing it renames the bot. |
| **Welcome line** | The first thing members read when they start the bot. |
| **Markets** | Which crypto perps your members can trade, and which show first. |
| **Leverage cap** | The highest leverage members can pick. |
| **Starting leverage** | The leverage picked first for new members. |
| **Largest order** | The biggest single order a member can place. |
| **Quick amounts** | The one-tap amounts on each market's card. |
| **Price limit (slippage)** | How far from the current price a market order may fill. It also decides when Long or Short is switched off for a thin order book. |
| **Owners** | Who sees your stats. We send each new owner a one-time invite link. |

The same rules apply in Telegram and in Lance Trade.

## Pausing your bot

We can pause your bot at your request. While it's paused:

- the bot still answers and tells members it's paused;
- members can't open new trades;
- it sends no alerts;
- members' resting orders, take profits and stop losses stay on Hyperliquid and can still fill;
- members can cancel orders and take profits and stop losses in Lance Trade, and sign browsers out.

Members can always manage their positions directly on Hyperliquid with their wallet.
