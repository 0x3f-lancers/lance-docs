Lance gives your community its own trading bot in Telegram, under your name and logo. Your members trade crypto perps on Hyperliquid without leaving your chat, or in Lance Trade in the browser. You earn 50% of Lance's bot fee on your community's trades.

## More than a referral link

A referral link sends your members to an exchange's website, under the exchange's brand. Lance puts trading inside your Telegram, under your name.

## Who it's for

- Futures trading groups on Telegram
- Trading educators and creators with an active trading audience
- Small wallets and exchanges that want a trading bot under their own brand
- Regional crypto media

Lance is built for perps traders. If your members mostly trade memecoins, it isn't the right fit yet.
