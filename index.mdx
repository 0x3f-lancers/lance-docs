Lance gives a trading community its own Telegram trading bot, under the community's own name. Members trade crypto perps on Hyperliquid from the chat, or from **Lance Trade**, a full trading screen in the browser. Lance is built by [Lancers Technology](https://lancers.technology).

<Note>
  Lance runs on Hyperliquid **testnet** for now, so trades use test funds with no real value. See [Is Lance live on mainnet?](/faq#is-lance-live-on-mainnet)
</Note>

## Your funds stay with you

Your money stays in your own Hyperliquid account. When you connect, you approve a trading key that can place and close trades for you. It can't withdraw funds or send them to another wallet, and you can remove it at any time. [How access works](/safety/how-access-works)

<Warning>
  Nobody from Lance or your community's bot will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key. Check every link against the [official links](/safety/official-links).
</Warning>
