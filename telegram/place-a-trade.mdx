## Home

Send `/start`, or tap **🏠 Home**, for your account, your open positions and a board of every market with its price, 24-hour change and funding rate. The board keeps updating for five minutes; tap **🔄 Refresh** after that.

## Open a market

Tap a market on Home, tap **📊 Markets** or send `/trade` for all of them. You can also just type its ticker, such as `btc`.

A market's card has:

- a candlestick chart, in 15m, 1h, 4h or 1d;
- the price, and your position in that market if you have one;
- quick **Long** and **Short** amounts, and a leverage switch;
- **✏️ Custom**, for any amount and leverage.

## Pick a size and leverage

Tap one of the quick amounts, or tap **✏️ Custom** and type an amount, with leverage if you like: `75` or `75 4x`. With Custom, you can also add a stop loss and take profit that the bot places as soon as the order fills.

Amounts are **position sizes**. The margin you put up is the size divided by the leverage: a $100 position at 4x uses $25 of margin.

Your community sets which markets you can trade, the highest leverage and the largest order. The bot tells you if an order is outside them.

## Review and confirm

Before anything is sent you see a review: the market and side, size, leverage, margin and the bot fee. Tap the ✅ button, which names the trade (for example **✅ Short BTC $20**), to place it, or tap **Cancel**.

The bot places a market order on Hyperliquid. It fills within your community's price limit (slippage) of the current price, or not at all. Then the bot shows the fill: the price, the size, Hyperliquid's fee and the bot fee.

<Note>
  Prefer no review step? Switch on one-tap trading in [Settings](/telegram/settings). Each tap on Long or Short then trades straight away.
</Note>

## When Long or Short is switched off

The bot checks Hyperliquid's order book before you trade. If nobody is selling (for a long) or buying (for a short), or the gap between buyers and sellers is wider than your community's price limit, that button is switched off and the card says why. Try a smaller size, or try again later.

## Your positions

Positions show on Home and on each market's card, with size, PnL, return and liquidation price. To close, open the position's card and tap **Close all** or **Close 50%**, or type `close btc` or `close btc 50%`.

Telegram places market orders. For limit orders, use [Lance Trade](/lance-trade/trade-on-the-web).
