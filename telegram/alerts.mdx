Your bot sends three kinds of alerts: about your positions, about prices you choose, and about [wallets you follow](/telegram/follow-wallets).

## Position alerts

Your bot watches your Hyperliquid account and messages you when:

| Alert | When |
| --- | --- |
| 🎯 **Take profit or stop loss filled** | One the bot placed fills. You get the size, price, realized PnL, Hyperliquid's fee and the bot fee. |
| **Exit couldn't fill** | A take profit or stop loss triggers but Hyperliquid can't fill it, for example when the price moves past its 10% slippage limit. Your position is still open. |
| **Close to liquidation** | A first warning as the price nears your liquidation price, and an urgent one when it gets very close. You hear again only after the position has moved away and back. |
| **Liquidated** | A position was liquidated. |

### Turn position alerts on or off

Open [Settings](/telegram/settings) and tap **🔕 Turn alerts off** or **🔔 Turn alerts on**. Exits the bot placed still work while alerts are off; you just don't get the messages.

## Price alerts

Get a message when a market reaches a price you pick.

- **In the bot:** tap **🔔 Alerts** on Home or **🔔 Alert** on a market's card. Pick a level such as **▲ +5%** or **▼ −2%** from the current price, or **✏️ Type a price**. You can also type it: `/alert btc 80000`, `/alert btc 2.5k` or `/alert btc -5%`. Send `/alerts` for all of yours.
- **In Lance Trade:** tap the 🔔 bell beside a market's price, or **Set price alert** in a position's ⋯ menu. Waiting alerts show as dashed lines on the chart.

Each alert fires once, in Telegram (and in Lance Trade too, if it's open), with the price now and how far it moved. Tap **🔔 Set another** for the next one. You can have up to 20 alerts waiting, each within a tenth and ten times the current price. An alert that hasn't fired after 30 days expires, and the bot tells you.

Your community's owners can switch price alerts off.

## Alerts can arrive late

The bot checks accounts every 10 seconds when a position is near liquidation, every 30 seconds when you have positions or exits, and every 5 minutes otherwise, and prices for price alerts every 5 seconds. Telegram can also be slow. Treat alerts as a convenience, not a safety net: set a stop loss on every position you can't watch.

<Note>
  If Lance pauses your community's bot, it sends no alerts until it's running again. Your take profits and stop losses stay on Hyperliquid and still work.
</Note>
