Send `/settings`, or tap **⚙️ Settings**. Lance Trade uses the same defaults, so a change in one shows in the other.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Order size** | The amount picked first on a market's card. |
| **Leverage** | The leverage picked first, within your community's cap. |
| **Chart** | The chart timeframe a card opens with: 15m, 1h, 4h or 1d. |
| **Trades** | **Confirm each one** shows a review before every trade. **⚡ One tap** trades as soon as you tap Long or Short. |
| **Alerts** | Messages about fills and liquidation risk. See [Alerts](/telegram/alerts). |
| **🕐 Time zone** | The time zone the bot writes times in. Until you set one, it uses UTC and says so. |

From Settings you can also open **🔐 Wallet and access**: your connected wallet, your trading key, Lance Trade sign-in and your devices. See [Wallet and access](/account/wallet-and-access).
