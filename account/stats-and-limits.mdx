## Your stats

Send `/mystats` for **📅 Your stats**, month by month. Only you see it.

| Figure | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Net** | Your result after fees and funding |
| **Realized PnL** | Your result before fees and funding |
| **Fees** | Hyperliquid's fees and the bot fee together |
| **Funding** | Funding paid and received |
| **Volume** | The size of all your trades |
| **Round trips** | Positions you opened and closed |
| **Average hold** | How long you held a position, on average |

It also splits your results by market and by long or short. Use ◀ and ▶ to move between months, and open a day to see its trades, write yourself a **📝 Note**, or **🖼 Share** it.

In Lance Trade, open **Portfolio**, then **Your stats**, for the last 7, 30 or 90 days.

## Net PnL on every close

When you close a position, the bot shows what it cost you:

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **PnL before fees** | The price move on your position |
| **Fee to open** | Hyperliquid's fee and the bot fee when you opened |
| **Fee to close** | The same when you closed |
| **Funding** | What you paid or received while it was open |
| **Net PnL** | What's left after all of them |

A figure marked "(est.)" is an estimate. For a partial close, the fee to open and the funding are that part's share. Lance Trade shows the same.

## Your limits

Send `/limits`, or tap **🧱 Your limits** in **⚙️ Settings**. In Lance Trade, it's **Your limits** in the Account sheet. Only you see them, and they apply in the bot and in Lance Trade.

| Limit | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **🧯 Daily loss limit** | Stops new trades once you've lost this much in the last 24 hours |
| **🎚 Max leverage** | The highest leverage you can pick, below your community's cap |
| **📏 Max order size** | The biggest single order you can place |
| **⏸ Pause after a liquidation** | No new trades for 1, 4 or 24 hours after a liquidation |
| **🪞 Max copy trades a day** | How many [copy trades](/telegram/follow-wallets#copy-a-trade) you can place a day |

Making a limit **tighter** applies at once. Making it **looser** waits 24 hours, so a bad moment can't undo it; **↩️ Keep my current limits** cancels the change. Closing a position, take profits, stop losses and cancelling orders always work.

Your community's owners may set limits for everyone too. You see them on the same screen.

## Share cards

Tap **🖼 Share** on a position or a day, or **🖼 Share result** on a closed trade, for an image to post. Before you share, choose:

- **💵 Show $** or hide your result in dollars (hidden at first);
- **🙈 Hide prices** or show them;
- **☀️ Light** or **🌙 Dark**;
- **🔗 Copy bot link**, to share your community's bot with it.

In Lance Trade, **Share a card** has the same choices. Your community's owners can switch share cards off.

<Warning>
  A card shows one result, not your whole record. Don't post it as a promise of what others will make.
</Warning>
