<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your community's bot">
    Use the bot link your community owner shares. Check the bot's username before you start: impersonators copy names and logos. Want to look around first? Try the example bot, [@Northstar123bot](https://telegram.me/Northstar123bot).

    Tap **Start**. The bot's Home screen shows your account, your positions and live market prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Have USDC in your Hyperliquid account">
    You trade from your own Hyperliquid account, so it needs USDC in it. While Lance is on testnet, tap **Get test USDC** in the bot for Hyperliquid's testnet faucet. The faucet only pays wallets that have deposited on Hyperliquid mainnet before.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Tap the connect button in the bot to open the approval page at `connect.lancetrade.xyz`. Check that it names your Telegram username, then connect your wallet and sign the steps it shows. Tap **Back to Telegram** when it says you're all set.

    Full walkthrough: [Connect your wallet](/telegram/connect-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your first trade">
    Tap a market, or send `/trade` to see them all. Pick an amount and tap **Long** or **Short**. Check the review, including the bot fee, then confirm.

    More: [Place a trade](/telegram/place-a-trade).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a take profit and stop loss">
    On the market's card, tap 🎯 for a take profit or 🛡 for a stop loss. You see the price and what you would make or lose before anything is placed.

    More: [Take profit and stop loss](/telegram/take-profit-stop-loss).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Lance Trade (optional)">
    Want a bigger screen? Tap **🌐 Lance Trade** on Home to get a one-time sign-in button for `app.lancetrade.xyz`. No password needed.

    More: [Sign in to Lance Trade](/lance-trade/sign-in).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Send `/help` at any time for a short guide inside the bot, including the trades you can type.
</Tip>
