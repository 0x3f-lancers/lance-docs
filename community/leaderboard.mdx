Your community's owners can switch on a leaderboard. When it's on, you find **🏆 Leaderboard** on Home in the bot (after **💼 Account**), `/leaderboard`, and **Leaderboard** in Lance Trade. When it's off, it's gone from both.

## Who's on it

Members with a wallet connected who traded through your community's bot in the last 30 days. Nobody sees your Telegram username: you show under the name you chose for [chart comments](/community/chart-comments), or an anonymous tag such as `Trader 7F3A`.

To change your name, tap **✏️ Your name** and send one: 3 to 20 letters, digits, `_` or `.`, with no spaces. The same name shows with your chart comments.

Don't want to be on it? Tap **🙈 Hide me** in the bot (**👁 Show me** brings you back), or use the switch on your card in Lance Trade.

## What counts

Your whole Hyperliquid perps account over the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time: open and closed positions, after fees and funding. Trades you made elsewhere, and liquidations, count too, so closing only winning trades doesn't move anyone up.

Your community's owners choose how it's ranked:

- **Return** (the default): your result over the money you had at work, from $50. The board shows percentages only.
- **PnL**: your result in dollars.

In Lance Trade, the first three stand on a podium, everyone else follows in a table, and your own card shows where you stand, or why you aren't ranked yet.

Owners can also have the top 10 posted in your community's Telegram group, daily or weekly.

<Warning>
  A leaderboard shows past results only. It says nothing about what anyone will make next, and it's no reason to copy someone or trade more. There are no prizes.
</Warning>
