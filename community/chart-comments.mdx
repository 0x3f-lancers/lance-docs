In Lance Trade, members of your community can comment on a market's chart. Only your own community's members see them. Your community's owners can switch comments off.

## Read and write comments

Open a market in Lance Trade and pick the **Comments** tab in the panel under the chart, on a tablet or desktop. You need to be signed in to write.

- **Pin to a candle** ties your comment to a point on the chart.
- React with 👍 💡 👀 or 😂, or **Reply**.
- The first time, you choose a name: 3 to 20 letters, digits, `_` or `.`. It's also your name on the [leaderboard](/community/leaderboard). Your Telegram username never shows.

## Rules

- Up to 280 characters, at least 15 seconds apart, and 60 a day.
- No links, @usernames, wallet addresses or seed phrases: comments with them are refused.
- Keep it about the market. Comments aren't advice, and nobody should treat them as a reason to trade.

## Report a comment

Tap **Report** on a comment that breaks the rules or looks like a scam. Your community's owners get it in Telegram, and can remove the comment or mute its writer. A muted member can still read comments.

<Warning>
  Nobody from Lance or your community will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key, in a comment or anywhere else. See [Official links and scams](/safety/official-links).
</Warning>
