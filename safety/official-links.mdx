## What we will never do

- Ask for your seed phrase or private key.
- Ask you to sign anything on any website other than `connect.lancetrade.xyz`.
- Ask you to send funds to anyone.

## Check before you sign

- The approval page's address is exactly `connect.lancetrade.xyz`.
- The page names **your** Telegram username before it asks for any signature.
- The signatures it asks for match [Connect your wallet](/telegram/connect-wallet): a message naming you and the bot, the trading key and the bot fee.

## If something looks wrong

1. Don't sign anything, and close the page.
2. In your bot, open **🔐 Wallet and access**. Tap **Remove access** if you think your access is at risk, and **Sign out everywhere** under **🖥 Devices**.
3. Report it to your community owner and to the [Lance team](https://telegram.me/bhargav2495).
