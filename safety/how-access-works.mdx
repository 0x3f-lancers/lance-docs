When you connect, you don't hand over your wallet. You approve a separate trading key on Hyperliquid (Hyperliquid calls it an API wallet) that only your community's bot holds.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Lance can" icon="check">
    - Place and close the trades you ask for
    - Set take profits and stop losses
    - Change leverage on a market
    - Charge its bot fee, up to the rate you approved
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lance can't" icon="xmark">
    - Withdraw your funds
    - Send funds to another wallet
    - See or use your wallet's own keys or seed phrase
    - Keep trading after you remove access, or after the key expires
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The trading key, in full

Hyperliquid lets an API wallet sign any trading action on your account. Besides trades, that includes moving funds between your own Hyperliquid balances and sub-accounts, or into vaults. It never includes withdrawing or sending funds to another wallet. Your bot only uses its key for the trades and settings you ask for.

## How keys are kept

- **One key per member.** Each member gets their own key, made for them when they connect. A key is never reused: reconnecting, renewing or removing access deletes the old one.
- **Encrypted.** Keys are stored encrypted, and never reach your browser or Lance Trade.
- **Short-lived.** Each key expires after at most 180 days. You renew it with one signature.

## Links tied to you

- An approval link names the Telegram user it's for, and the page shows it before any signature.
- The bot only connects the wallet that signed, and only for a one-time code sent from that same Telegram account. A forwarded link can't connect someone else's wallet to your account.
- Lance Trade sign-in links work once, for 10 minutes, and every new browser is announced in your chat.

## Trades stay private

You trade in a private chat with the bot, or in your own Lance Trade session, never in a group. Nobody can trade for you by posting a command in your community's group.

## Take access back at any time

- In the bot: **🔐 Wallet and access**, then **Remove access**. See [Remove access](/account/wallet-and-access#remove-access).
- On Hyperliquid: revoke the bot's key yourself, without going through the bot.

<Warning>
  No software is free of risk. Use a wallet you're comfortable trading from, keep your seed phrase offline, and only use the [official links](/safety/official-links).
</Warning>
