## Connecting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The approval page says the link has expired">
    Approval links work for 30 minutes, and stop once you've approved or the bot gives you a newer one. Tap **Get a fresh link** on the page, or send `/start connect` to your bot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Telegram didn't open after I approved">
    The page shows a one-time code such as `ABCD-EFGH`. Send it to your bot from your own Telegram account. It works for 60 minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The page asks me to deposit first">
    Hyperliquid has never seen this wallet. Deposit USDC on Hyperliquid with it first, then open the link again. On testnet, the faucet only pays wallets that have deposited on Hyperliquid mainnet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The page asks for my main wallet">
    You connected an API wallet, a vault or a sub-account. Switch your wallet to the main account you use on Hyperliquid.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The page asks me to use a regular wallet">
    Smart-contract wallets can't connect, because the bot can't check their signatures. Use a regular wallet such as MetaMask.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There's no wallet on my phone">
    On a phone, a wallet only works inside its own app's browser. The approval page offers to reopen itself in MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet or Bitget Wallet. With MetaMask, you can also tap **🦊 Open in MetaMask** in the bot. No wallet app at all? Use a [Lance wallet](/account/lance-wallet).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The page says Lance isn't available where I am">
    Connecting a wallet and signing in to Lance Trade are refused from the countries Lance isn't offered in. See [Restricted regions](/legal/restricted-regions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Lance wallet

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Face ID or my passkey doesn't work on iPhone">
    Telegram's own browser on iPhone can't use Face ID or passkeys. Open the wallet page in Safari or Chrome: use the ⋯ menu to open it in Safari, or tap **📋 Copy link** in the bot and paste it there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I sent money but trading isn't on yet">
    The bot checks new Lance wallets every minute. Tap **🔄 Check now** on your **⚡ Lance wallet** screen to check straight away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I lost my phone">
    Any other device you added can still withdraw. If you lost your Telegram account too, see [Lost your Telegram account?](/account/lance-wallet#lost-your-telegram-account).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got a message that my wallet is moving">
    Someone used one of your wallet's passkeys to move it to another Telegram account. If it wasn't you, tap **🛑 Cancel the move** within 24 hours, then check who can use your devices.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Trading

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Long or Short button is switched off">
    Hyperliquid's order book can't fill that side right now: nobody is selling (for a long) or buying (for a short), or the gap is wider than your community's price limit. The card says which. Try a smaller size, or try again later.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My order is outside my community's limits">
    Your community sets the markets, the highest leverage and the largest order. Pick a smaller size or lower leverage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A trade was refused because my key expired">
    Your trading key lasts up to 180 days. Follow the renewal steps the bot shows, or see [Renew your trading key](/account/wallet-and-access#renew-your-trading-key).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Lance Trade says to use a different leverage">
    Hyperliquid keeps one leverage per market. With a position or resting orders in that market, new orders must use the same leverage. Tap **Use Nx** where it's offered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My bot says new orders are paused">
    Your community's owners have paused new orders. Closing positions, take profits, stop losses and cancelling orders work as usual. Resting orders stay on Hyperliquid and can still fill.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My bot says it's paused">
    Lance has paused your community's bot. You can still close positions and cancel orders in Lance Trade, and sign browsers out. Resting orders stay on Hyperliquid and can still fill. You can always manage your positions directly on Hyperliquid with your wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My bot says to join a group to trade">
    Your community only lets members of its Telegram group open trades. Join the group, then tap **✅ I've joined, check again**. Closing and cancelling work as usual.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A trade was refused because of a limit">
    It's either a risk limit your community's owners set, or one of [your own limits](/account/stats-and-limits#your-limits). The message says which, and when the order fits again. Closing always works.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Lance Trade

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The sign-in link doesn't work">
    Sign-in links work once, for 10 minutes. Tap **🌐 New link** in the bot, or **Get a new link** in Lance Trade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was signed out">
    Sessions end after 30 days, when you sign out, when you remove access or connect a different wallet, or when a browser is signed out from **🖥 Devices**. Sign in again from your bot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see a sign-in I don't recognise">
    Tap **Sign out this browser** on the bot's message, or **Sign out everywhere** in **🖥 Devices**. If you think someone has your Telegram account, also tap **Remove access**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
