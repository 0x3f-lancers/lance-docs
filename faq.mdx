### What is Lance?

A Telegram trading bot that a community runs under its own name, plus Lance Trade, a trading screen in the browser. Lance runs the technology, and trades execute on Hyperliquid. Lance is built by Lancers Technology.

### Is Lance live on mainnet?

Not yet. Lance runs on Hyperliquid testnet for now, so trades use test funds with no real value. Your bot and Lance Trade say "testnet" where it matters. These docs will say when that changes.

### Who runs my community's bot?

Your community owner chose the bot's name, welcome message and markets, and can set limits such as the highest leverage. Lance runs the bot and the technology behind it.

### Does Lance hold my money?

No. Your funds stay in your own Hyperliquid account. Lance can place the trades you ask for, but it can never withdraw or send your funds to another wallet. See [How access works](/safety/how-access-works).

### What do I pay?

Hyperliquid's trading fee plus Lance's bot fee of 0.05% per trade (illustrative; final fees are confirmed before launch). The bot fee is shown before every trade. See [Fees](/account/fees).

### Does Lance tell me what to trade?

No. You make every trade yourself. Lance doesn't give advice, manage money or trade on anyone's behalf.

### Which markets can I trade?

Crypto perpetual futures on Hyperliquid, from the list your community has chosen.

### Which wallets work?

Your main Hyperliquid wallet, in a browser extension such as MetaMask, or on a phone in MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet or Bitget Wallet. Smart-contract wallets can't connect. See [Connect your wallet](/telegram/connect-wallet#what-you-need).

### Can I trade without a wallet app?

Yes, with a [Lance wallet](/account/lance-wallet): a new wallet for your community's bot that only your phone's lock (PIN, Face ID or fingerprint) can take money out of. Lance can't withdraw from it. Connecting your own wallet is still the most secure way, and the one we recommend for serious trading.

### Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Telegram works anywhere, Lance Trade works in any phone browser, and the approval page reopens inside your wallet app's browser. A [Lance wallet](/account/lance-wallet) needs no wallet app at all.

### Which countries can use Lance?

Lance isn't available to people in the United States, the United Kingdom or sanctioned countries. Connecting a wallet and signing in to Lance Trade are refused from those countries. See [Restricted regions](/legal/restricted-regions).

### How do I stop using Lance?

Tap **Remove access** in **🔐 Wallet and access**. The bot can no longer trade for you, and every Lance Trade browser is signed out. To also take Lance's approvals off your wallet on Hyperliquid, use the remove page the bot links to. See [Remove access](/account/wallet-and-access#remove-access).

### Someone asked for my seed phrase. What do I do?

Don't share it. Nobody from Lance or your community's bot will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key, and Lance never asks you to sign anything on any other website. See [Official links and scams](/safety/official-links).
